# Great Ideas

## LunaTick - Blind Lunar Mood Correlation Tracker
- **Core Concept**: A discreet mood survey that intentionally obscures its true purpose from respondents ("this is a test please trust me and answer").
- **Secret Metric**: Automatically computes and records the current Moon Phase (e.g., New Moon, Waxing Crescent, Full Moon, Waning Gibbous) alongside the user's name, timestamp, and selected mood card into a Google Sheet.
- **Value**: Prevents placebo/observer bias in testing whether moon phases affect friends' or teammates' moods and emotional patterns.
- **Tone & Mood Language**: Use casual, relatable everyday language ("On a roll today", "Mood off", "Cruising / Chill", "Chaos mode", "Low battery") instead of clinical psychology jargon to get honest, unfiltered gut responses.
